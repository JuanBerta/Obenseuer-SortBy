=== NAME ===
Sort By

=== SHORT DESCRIPTION (summary) ===
Sort your inventory, the bank, the backpack or any storage by value, name, amount, quality or freshness, with hotkeys to sort without clicking. Fast even on a huge multi-page bank.

=== FILE DESCRIPTION ===
Obenseuer-SortBy-v1.0.2.rar : Sort By 1.0.2 for BepInEx 6. Extract into the Obenseuer folder.

=== FULL DESCRIPTION (BBCode) ===
[size=5][b]Sort By[/b][/size]
[i]Choose how your inventory and storages are sorted, and sort them with a key press.[/i]

[size=4][b]Features[/b][/size]
[list]
[*][b]Sort by menu[/b]: the round [b]Sort[/b] buttons (your inventory, and any open storage: bank, backpack, containers) open a small menu:
[list]
[*]Category (the game's own order)
[*]Name
[*]Value (each)
[*]Value (stack) - price x amount
[*]Amount
[*]Quality
[*]Freshness
[*]Reverse order
[/list]
Pick one and it sorts right away. Your choice is remembered.
[*][b]Hotkeys[/b] while the inventory is open:
[list]
[*][b]U[/b]: sort your inventory
[*][b]Y[/b]: sort the storage under the mouse (your inventory, the bank, the backpack, a container...)
[/list]
[*][b]Fast on big storages[/b]: the game's sort gets slow on a bank with hundreds of slots. Storages with 100+ slots use a faster routine with the same order and the same stack merging, so there's no freeze.
[*]Equipment slots are never sorted. Sorting uses the game's own item handling, so stacks, liquids, quality and freshness are kept.
[/list]

[size=4][b]Configuration[/b][/size]
[code]BepInEx\config\com.juanberta.obenseuer.sortby.cfg[/code]
[list]
[*][b]Sorting[/b]: SortBy, Reverse, SortButtonOpensMenu (off = the Sort buttons sort right away with your order), FastSortFromSlots
[*][b]Keys[/b]: SortInventoryKey (U), SortHoveredKey (Y)
[/list]

[size=4][b]Compatibility[/b][/size]
[list]
[*]Works with [b]Bank Inventory[/b]: sort the whole bank (all pages) from its Sort button or with Y.
[*]Safe to add or remove at any time; it doesn't touch your save.
[/list]

[size=4][b]Installation[/b][/size]
[list=1]
[*]Install [b]BepInEx 6[/b] (x64).
[*]Extract the archive into your Obenseuer folder (where Obenseuer.exe is).
[/list]
